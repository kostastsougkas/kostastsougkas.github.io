Hi! I’m Kostas, a mathematician from Greece. I earned my PhD in mathematics at Uppsala University, having also spent one semester at Cornell University as an exchange graduate student. Currently, I work at the University of Skövde. My research focuses on analysis on fractal spaces, particularly the relationship between discrete graphs and continuous structures.

University of Skövde

Analysis onfractal spaces

I study how ideas from mathematical analysis and discrete mathematics interact on fractal spaces.

My work concerns Laplace operators on self-similar fractals and the finite graphs that approximate them. This includes harmonic structures, spectral zeta functions, regularized determinants, energy measures, and spanning-tree asymptotics.

Teaching is my life's passion.

As an undergraduate, I worked part-time as a private tutor. At university level, I have experience teaching single- and multivariable calculus, linear algebra, transform methods, discrete mathematics, and mathematics for engineers. I also have experience teaching a range of middle- and high-school courses in the Swedish and Greek curricula. Beyond mathematics, I have taught Bachata, a very popular type of Latin dance.

I have also given popular-science talks at high schools on fractals, infinity, and other cool mathematical topics, aiming to inspire students and spark an interest in science. I am always happy to promote mathematics through outreach. If you would like me to speak at your school, university, or event about fractals or another engaging mathematical topic, please get in touch.
